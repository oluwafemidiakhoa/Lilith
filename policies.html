BOOKING & CLASS POLICIES

Clear expectations, with room for real life.

These policies are designed to protect Therry's preparation time while keeping the customer experience fair and understandable.

Advance booking

Private readings must be booked at least 48 hours in advance. Available appointment times will be the blocks Therry chooses to open on her booking calendar.

Appointment buffers

Live private appointments are planned with a 15-minute buffer between customers so there is time to reset and prepare for the next session.

Cancellation

Please provide at least 24 hours' notice to cancel. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice are non-refundable, except when Therry chooses to make an exception for a genuine emergency.

Rescheduling

Appointments may be rescheduled with at least 24 hours' notice. Genuine emergencies may be handled differently at Therry's discretion.

No-shows

A missed appointment without notice is treated as a late cancellation and is normally non-refundable, unless Therry decides an emergency exception is appropriate.

If Therry must cancel

If Sweet Tea Astrology must cancel a paid appointment or class, the customer will be offered a reschedule, transfer, or refund rather than losing the payment.

Technical problems

If a significant technical problem on Sweet Tea Astrology's side prevents a paid live service from being delivered, the session will be rescheduled or another fair remedy will be offered.

Monthly classes

Class registration closes when the event reaches its stated capacity or the published registration deadline. The capacity and deadline will be shown before registration opens.

Recording and public use

Attendees will be informed if a class is recorded. A recording used for private class/replay purposes does not automatically authorize public use. Identifiable attendee names, voices, faces, or personal chart information will not be intentionally published in YouTube, social media, or promotional content without separate permission.

Astrology disclaimer: Sweet Tea Astrology services are for personal reflection and entertainment. They are not substitutes for medical, legal, financial, or mental-health advice.
