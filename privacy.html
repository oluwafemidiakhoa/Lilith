PRIVACY & DATA CARE

Your information should be treated with care.

Sweet Tea Astrology is designed to collect only the information needed to provide a reading, class, booking, payment, or reminder. We do not sell customer information.

Information we may collect

When booking opens, a customer may provide a name, email address, optional mobile number, birth date, birth time, birthplace, reading question or topic, and reminder preference. Relationship readings may also require birth information for a second person.

How information is used

Information is used to prepare and deliver astrology services, schedule appointments, process payments, send confirmations or reminders, provide class access, respond to customer requests, and maintain necessary business records.

Payments and scheduling

Sweet Tea Astrology plans to use established third-party services for scheduling, payment processing, calendar coordination, video meetings, and transactional messages. Payment card details should be entered directly with the payment provider and should not be sent to Sweet Tea Astrology by email or text.

Text-message consent

If SMS reminders are offered, customers will be able to choose whether to receive them. Providing a phone number does not by itself enroll someone in marketing messages. Transactional reminders will be sent only to customers who select text reminders, and normal opt-out instructions will apply.

Class recordings

If a live class is recorded, attendees will be informed. A class recording used privately for the class is different from public use. Identifiable attendee names, voices, faces, or personal chart information will not be intentionally used in public promotional or YouTube material without separate permission.

Retention and deletion

Customer information should be kept only as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, administer the business, resolve payment issues, or meet applicable recordkeeping requirements. Customers may request correction or deletion of information that Sweet Tea Astrology directly controls, subject to records that must be retained for legitimate business or legal reasons.

Security

Sweet Tea Astrology aims to minimize sensitive data, use secure hosted providers for payment and scheduling, restrict account access, and avoid storing payment credentials on this website. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to therry@sweetteastrology.com.
