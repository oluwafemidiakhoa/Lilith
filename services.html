01
BIRTH CHART READING

Birth Chart Reading

Who Am I?

A personal astrology reading centered around one of life’s biggest questions: Who am I?

Your birth chart maps your unique energies. Together, we’ll explore your strengths, challenges, recurring patterns, and the parts of yourself seeking greater expression.

We’ll look at how to work with these energies consciously, better understand yourself, make authentic choices, and create more happiness and fulfillment in your life.

02
CURRENT TRANSITS

Current Transits

Why Is This Happening Now?

Life makes more sense when you can see the pattern.

A transit reading looks at the planetary energies influencing you right now and the areas of your life being activated. We’ll explore why certain themes may be coming to the surface and how long these influences will last.

Together, we will explore how to use the current energy patterns to your advantage and create more joy in the season of life you are in.

03
RELATIONSHIP & SYNASTRY

Relationship & Synastry

Why Does This Person Affect Me This Way?

Every relationship creates its own unique energy.

A synastry reading compares two birth charts to explore the connections between you. We’ll look at where attraction and understanding come naturally, where tension or misunderstandings may arise, and the deeper patterns that draw you together.

Together, we’ll explore what each of you brings out in the other and how understanding those patterns can help you navigate the relationship with greater awareness, connection, and joy.

04
BIRTHDAY / SOLAR RETURN

Birthday / Solar Return Reading

What Is This Year About?

Every birthday begins a new astrological year and brings a new set of energies into focus.

A Solar Return reading explores the themes and opportunities of the year ahead. We’ll look at your birthday chart along with your birth chart and important transits to see which areas of your life are being emphasized and where change, growth, or new possibilities may be emerging.

Together, we’ll explore how you can work with the energies of your year more intentionally—helping you recognize opportunities, navigate challenges, and create more of what you want in the year ahead.

05
PURPOSE & DIRECTION

Purpose & Direction Reading

Where Am I Going?

What kind of life feels meaningful to me?

A Purpose & Direction reading explores the parts of your birth chart that speak to your natural gifts, motivations, and the path of action that will help you feel most fulfilled and happy in life.

Together, we’ll look for the threads that run through your chart and connect them into a bigger picture—helping you understand what gives your life meaning, where and how you may feel called to grow, and how you can create a life that feels more authentically your own.
