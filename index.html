MEET THERRY

From curiosity to a lifelong practice.

In my twenties I read a book that claimed you could know your purpose in life if you could read your birth chart in astrology. Like many people in their twenties trying to figure out who they are and where they are going in life, I was fascinated by this idea.

I couldn’t afford a professional reading, so I bought a couple of astrology books and began learning for myself. That curiosity grew into more than 25 years of practicing astrology, studying charts, and helping others understand the patterns unfolding in their own lives.

I don’t believe astrology should tell you how to live your life. I believe it should help you understand the landscape so you can navigate your life more intentionally.
